A named select takes part in a form: the popover variant submits its value through a hidden input, and the native variant's `<select>` carries `name`. Select options are 44px rows, and the `data-bn` sidebar's links are 44px and stack above the content on a phone. site: the enterprise example renders its components as markup (they printed as escaped text), its New User form posts, and its role picker is the custom select, and it links the catalog sheet so its controls are styled.
