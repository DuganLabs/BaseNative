Catalog review fixes. The app nav's keyboard sync is hardened: `initAppNav` now re-syncs on focus (the earliest keyboard signal, plus a settle pass), tracks the largest visual-viewport inset measured while a text-entry control holds focus — iOS reports the viewport in stages during a keyboard opening, and trusting the last report could leave the bar sunk back into the keyboard for the whole episode — and clears only when the keyboard is fully gone. Tooltips are placed with `position-area` and `position-try` span fallbacks, so a tooltip at a screen edge re-centres into the half that has room instead of painting off-screen or crushing into a sliver; the tooltip trigger joins the shared button-reset group (it previously rendered with user-agent button chrome) and the 24px target-size floor, and reads as plain text with a dotted underline. Dropdown menus gain flip position-try fallbacks for edge triggers. Avatars carry a `data-tone` (a stable hash of the name, 0–7) with a lit, ringed disc per tone, replacing the identical flat grey disc for every person. On coarse pointers the control-height ladder's default rung is 44px (2.75rem). Pipeline card titles/subtitles and calendar event titles no longer break words mid-word (`overflow-wrap: anywhere` removed). The drawer can no longer cover a sub-320px viewport edge to edge.
